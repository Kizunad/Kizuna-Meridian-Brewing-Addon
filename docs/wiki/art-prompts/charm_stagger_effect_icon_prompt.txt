Use case: game asset. A single Minecraft Java Edition style status effect icon, the kind shown next to the hotbar and in the inventory when a potion effect is active. Authentic vanilla pixel art: design as exactly 16 by 16 logical pixels, enlarged to 1024 by 1024 with nearest-neighbor, every logical pixel a large flat square block, crisp stair-step edges, no antialiasing, no gradients, no dithering noise, no photorealism, no painterly marks, no 3D render. Match the look of vanilla Minecraft mob effect icons such as Strength, Poison or Regeneration: one simple bold symbol, light from the top left, 3 to 5 flat shades of one main hue, a one-pixel outline drawn in a darker shade of the symbol's own color (not pure black), readable at 18 by 18 pixels, filling most of the 16 by 16 grid with one pixel of breathing room. The whole background is one flat pure magenta #FF00FF with no shadow, no floor, no frame, no vignette; never use magenta inside the symbol. Exactly one symbol. No text, letters, numbers, runes, watermark, logo, UI labels or border. Very important: the drawing must really be only 16 by 16 big pixels; each big pixel is a 64 by 64 square of one flat color, so the whole symbol is built from at most about 150 big squares and at most 7 distinct colors. Do not draw any detail smaller than one big pixel.
The symbol: a pink-violet heart tilted to one side with a jagged crack through it and three tiny pale stars spinning around its top, like being dazed. Pink-violet palette around #C77DBA with pale yellow stars.
